About Me

I discovered my passion for design at a young age. As I began exploring drawing and creating visuals for people and objects around me, my interest naturally grew deeper. Over time, that early curiosity stayed with me and continued to shape who I am. After completing high school, I came to understand that design was more than just a passion—it was something I was naturally inclined toward. Learning and improving didn’t feel difficult, because I already had a strong foundation and intuition for it. From there, I focused on refining my skills, and through consistent effort and growth, I developed into a professional graphic designer.
