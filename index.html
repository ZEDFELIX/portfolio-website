03 — About

Design didn’t choose me first — I couldn’t stop drawing it until it became the only thing that made sense.

I discovered my passion for design early, sketching people and things around me before I understood why. That instinct never left. After high school I stopped treating design as a hobby and started treating it as a skill — refining my eye, my process and my taste until making felt as natural as thinking.

Today I work across identity, interface and campaign design — multidisciplinary by nature, obsessive about the details that make work feel finished, not just done.
